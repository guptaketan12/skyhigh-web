About

Most developers learn websites. I learned the business behind one.

For four years, I built and ran a D2C business — as the person responsible for whether the whole thing worked. That teaches you something no brief can.

A website isn't finished when it's deployed. That's when it starts working, or doesn't.

It's where people discover. Compare. Hesitate. Trust. Buy. Come back — or leave for good.

I build with all of that in the room — not as an afterthought, but as the actual starting point.

Business first. Code second.

“Most customers will never meet your business. They will only ever meet your website — and they decide in seconds.”
