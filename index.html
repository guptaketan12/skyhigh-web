How I think

I care about this before I care about code.

01
What are you selling?
Not just the product. The promise, the reason it matters, and what a customer needs to understand before they care.
02
Who are you selling to?
Because a site for a jewellery brand shouldn't behave like a site for a cable manufacturer. Context changes everything.
03
Why should they trust you?
Trust is designed into hierarchy, imagery, proof, product information, clarity and the details people barely notice.
04
What should happen next?
Buy. Enquire. Call. Explore. Book. The best websites know the next move before the visitor has to think about it.
